## How each subset is scored (forward only)

- **Forward** means `temporal_class == FWD`, no `duplicate_of` flag, and a resolution date that has already passed. It covers the ledger plus the demo-arc and 2026 NBA-draft staging files.
- **Directional hit** means p > 0.5 and the outcome was YES, or p < 0.5 and the outcome was NO.
- **Baselines:**
  - attention: a constant forecaster set at the subset's realized hit rate. This is in-sample, so it is generous to the baseline.
  - Forbes: last-year persistence, using Chorus's own confidence level with the direction taken from the prior-year Forbes list. Two rows have no prior-year list checked (NBA players in the athletes top 10; Barkley in the top 50), and those get 0.5.

  - calendar: shown separately and kept out of pooled skill, because these are near-certain schedule checks with p of 0.97 to 0.99.
